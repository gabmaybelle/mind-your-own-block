# Mind Your Own Block

A Fabric and NeoForge mod for Minecraft 26.3. **Blocks that keep the shape you placed them in.**

You place a fence post and it joins the fence next to it. You place a stair and it bends into a
corner. You place a lantern post in a pond and it fills with water. Hold a key while you place, and
the block stays exactly as you placed it and minds its own business until you break it.

## What it does

- **Hold your mouse's side button while placing.** It's its own button, not sneak, so it works on
  scaffolding and while you sneak along a roof edge. No side button, or on a touchpad? Pick any key
  in Controls, under Mind Your Own Block.
- **No joining.** Fences, walls, glass panes and iron bars stand alone, including modded ones built
  on vanilla's.
- **Straight stairs.** Stairs never bend into an inner or outer corner.
- **Dry blocks.** Blocks placed into water stay dry. Buckets work as normal.
- **Stays put.** The block keeps its shape when its neighbours change, until it's broken. Break it
  and place it again to reset.

No new blocks or items, and nothing to set up. Both the server and the player need the mod; on a
server without it the key simply does nothing.
