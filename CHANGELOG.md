# Changelog

## 0.2.0+1.21.1

- Everything in 0.2.0, on Minecraft 1.21.1 (Fabric and NeoForge). Nothing else changes: the same
  button, the same shapes, the same padlock.

## 0.2.0

- A fence, wall, pane or iron bars placed normally beside one you placed with the button now ends
  its line at its own post instead of reaching toward it, and a stair placed normally beside one
  doesn't bend into a corner because of it. The lone block really stands on its own. Fences from
  0.1.0 that reach toward a lone post let go the next time anything beside them changes.
- Lighter on the game: fences, walls, panes and stairs far from any block placed with the button
  cost next to nothing extra when they change shape.

## 0.1.0

- A button to hold while placing: the mouse's side button to start with (change it in Controls,
  under Mind Your Own Block). It's its own button, not sneak, so it works on scaffolding and while
  sneaking.
- Holding it while placing: fences, walls, glass panes and iron bars don't join their neighbours
  (modded ones built on vanilla's too), stairs stay straight instead of bending into a corner, and
  blocks placed into water stay dry. A water bucket still fills a dry block afterwards.
- A small padlock beside the crosshair while it's held, so you know.
- Blocks placed into water with it stay dry, even in the middle of a pond, where the water around
  would otherwise pour back in.
- Blocks placed that way stay put: a fence, wall, pane or stair keeps its shape when you build
  around it later, until you break it. A fence placed beside it still reaches toward it, and water
  poured in still fills it. A block pushed by a piston goes back to normal.
