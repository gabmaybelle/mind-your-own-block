# Progress

## Where things stand

- **What it does:** hold the key (the mouse's side button by default) while placing: fences,
  walls, panes and bars don't join their neighbours, stairs stay straight, and blocks placed into
  water stay dry, even in a pond. A small padlock shows beside the crosshair while it's held. Those
  blocks stay put while you build around them, until broken (or pushed by a piston); a bucket still
  fills a dry one. A normal fence, wall, pane or bars beside one ends its line at its own post
  instead of reaching toward it, and a normal stair doesn't bend because of one. Fabric and NeoForge
  on 26.3 (`main`) and 1.21.1 (this branch).
- **Broken:** nothing known.
- **Next:** push, CI with Build Better and Macaw's, then
  the 0.2.0+1.21.1 release and its CurseForge files. Later shapes (gates, chests, redstone dust)
  only if players ask; 1.20.1 only if players ask.
